# Key terms

Page numbers are `[[page N]]` markers in this workspace's `source.txt`. The first table tracks the words whose meaning carries the book's argument or shifts between chapters; it is hand-merged from the notes. The second is the full ledger, gathered from each note's own key-terms table. Definitions follow Feynman's usage (or the preface writers', where marked), not a textbook's. Equations are images in this edition and are missing, so definitions that depend on a formula are given in words.
