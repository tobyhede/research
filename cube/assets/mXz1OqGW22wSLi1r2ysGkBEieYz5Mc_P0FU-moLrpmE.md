**Defined at** p. p. 143

Two kinds: limited security against severe privation, available to all outside the market, and absolute security of a particular income or position, available only to some.

**Evolves / reused at**

- ch09 p. 148 (the second, generalised, is the security of the barracks)
- ch14 p. 213 (a uniform minimum, on condition that group privileges lapse)
