What separates a free country from one under arbitrary government, and can collectivist economic planning be carried on within that limit? Hayek's answer: the Rule of Law, meaning government bound by general rules fixed in advance, is the mark of a free country; planning needs discretionary decisions between particular people and so cannot observe it; and any policy aiming at substantive equality or distributive justice is incompatible with it.
