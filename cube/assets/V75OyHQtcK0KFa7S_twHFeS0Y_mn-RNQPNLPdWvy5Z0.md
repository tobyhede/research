- The Bibliographical Note offers a reading list as evidence that the view is shared: Cassel, Chamberlin, Graham, Gregory, Halevy, Hayek's own Collectivist Economic Planning, Hutt, Lippmann, Mises (two titles), Muir, Polanyi, Queeny, Rappard, Robbins (three titles), Roepke (two titles), Rougier, Voigt, and five Chicago Public Policy Pamphlets by Simons, Gideonse, Hermens, Sulzbach and Heilperin (pp. 236-237). This is evidence of company, not of truth. The phrase that these writers reached their conclusions independently (p. 236) is not supported; my observation is that several are close associates of Hayek (Mises, Robbins, Roepke).
- The list includes titles dated 1944 (Mises's Omnipotent Government, Roepke's Civitas Humana) (pp. 236-237), so it was current to the year of publication.
