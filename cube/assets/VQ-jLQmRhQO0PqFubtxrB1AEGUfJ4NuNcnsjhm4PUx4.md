**A single plan needs a single ranking of everyone's ends, and no such ranking exists.** So the ranking has to be imposed. This is the engine of the whole book. [Ch 5, line 1242](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1242)

- **Author's claim:** terms such as the common good are too vague to direct a plan. Directing all economic activity requires a complete ranking of every competing need, and neither such a code nor a mind able to hold it exists. [Ch 5, line 1242](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1242) [Ch 5, line 1250](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1250)
- **Reasoning:** people may agree to plan without agreeing on what for. A majority assembly cannot then produce one coherent plan, so it hands the choice of ends to experts, and when separate boards still fail to add up to one plan, the demand grows for a single authority. [Ch 5, line 1260](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1260) [Ch 5, line 1270](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1270) [Ch 5, line 1282](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1282)
- **Evidence:** quotations from planners who themselves wanted Parliament to delegate wide powers (the Webbs, Laski), and a short reading of the last Weimar governments. [Ch 5, line 1264](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1264) [Ch 5, line 1284](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1284)
- **Boundary:** the argument applies to comprehensive planning. Hayek says the state indirectly controls almost everything once its share passes a certain proportion, but never says what proportion. [Ch 5, line 1258](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1258) **[Analysis]** That gap is where the argument's reach over mixed economies is decided.
- **Application [Application]:** before backing a programme, ask whether it needs people to agree on a ranking of ends or only on a rule. The more of the first it needs, the more it will rely on delegation and discretion.
