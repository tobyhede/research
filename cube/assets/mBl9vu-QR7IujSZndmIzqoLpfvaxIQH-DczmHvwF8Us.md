**Defined at** p. p. 81

The genus of which socialism is a species: central direction of economic activity, whatever distributive end it serves.

**Evolves / reused at**

- ch05 p. 97 (organising all of society for one goal, recognising no autonomous sphere of individual ends)
- ch10 p. 155 (used almost interchangeably with totalitarianism)
- ch10 p. 158 (necessarily particularist: national, racial or class)
