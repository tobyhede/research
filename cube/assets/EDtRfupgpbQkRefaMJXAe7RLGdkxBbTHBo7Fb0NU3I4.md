**Control of the economy is control of the means to every end.** There is no separate, merely economic sphere that can be handed over safely. [Ch 7, line 1428](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1428)

---

- **Author's claim:** money matters because it lets the holder choose which of his ends to serve. Whoever controls all economic means decides which ends are met, for consumers and still more for people choosing an occupation. [Ch 7, line 1420](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1420) [Ch 7, line 1438](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1438)
- **Reasoning:** under competition an obstacle is impersonal, and a refusal by one supplier or employer can be answered by going to another. A single planning authority is a monopolist over everything. State ownership therefore creates a power that nobody holds while ownership is dispersed. [Ch 7, line 1434](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1434) [Ch 8, line 1494](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1494)
- **Evidence:** mostly mechanism and thought experiment. The empirical support is thin: second-hand figures on Soviet income inequality, and exchange control as the first step on the Continent. [Ch 8, line 1574](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1574) [Ch 7, line 1472](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1472)
- **Boundary:** Hayek concedes that the price of choice in a competitive society is often cruelly high and that the poor have far fewer options. [Ch 7, line 1448](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1448) [Ch 8, line 1490](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1490) He discusses limited occupational choice, mobility and alternative employment arrangements. [Ch 7, line 1440](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1440) [Ch 7, line 1442](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1442) [Ch 7, line 1444](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1444) **[Analysis]** He does not establish empirically how adequate those alternatives are for workers with few resources.
- **Application [Application]:** judge a control by whether those subject to it can go elsewhere.
