**Defined at** p. p. 82

The target: central direction of all economic activity according to a single plan. Rational foresight in general is not objected to.

**Evolves / reused at**

- ch03 p. 87 (planning for competition is accepted, planning against it is the target)
- ch09 p. 145 (planning in the good sense, such as monetary policy)
- ch04 p. 91 (stretched to cover German cartel and tariff policy)
- ch08 p. 133 (partial planning as a step that cannot stop)
- ch13 p. 190 (equated with organisation)
- ch15 p. 230 (a looser, approved sense: desirable planning done locally)
