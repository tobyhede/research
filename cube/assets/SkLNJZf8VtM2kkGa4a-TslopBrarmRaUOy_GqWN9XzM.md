All items are **[Application]**, derived from tests the book itself states.

- **Try now:** when assessing a policy, ask four questions. Is it a general rule announced in advance, or a power to decide cases? [Ch 6, line 1326](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1326) Does it fix particular prices, quantities or entry to a trade? [Ch 3, line 1134](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1134) Does it give security as a floor for everyone, or as protection for one group's position? [Ch 9, line 1606](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1606) Does it need agreement on whose needs rank higher? [Ch 5, line 1254](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1254)
- **Conditions required:** the tests presuppose working competition and a sound legal framework, which Hayek says the state must build and maintain. [Ch 3, line 1138](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1138)
- **Warning signs:** statutes that hand boards open-ended discretion under words like fair or reasonable; capital and labour in one industry jointly seeking protection; a single goal declared worth any price. [Ch 6, line 1344](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1344) [Ch 13, line 2088](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L2088) [Ch 14, line 2242](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L2242)
- **Failure modes of the book's own advice:** the tests sort kinds of intervention but give no threshold, and Hayek admits the formal distinction blurs in practice. [Ch 6, line 1354](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1354)
- **Seven-day experiment:** take one policy proposal currently in the news and one you already support. Run the four questions on each and write down where the answers differ from your first reaction.
