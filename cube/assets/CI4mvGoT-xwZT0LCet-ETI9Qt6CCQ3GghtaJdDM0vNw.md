- **Author's claim:** the worst features of totalitarian regimes are products of the system. The group large and uniform enough to impose one view tends to be united by low common standards, by credulity, and by hatred of an enemy. [Ch 10, line 1690](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1690) [Ch 10, line 1704](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1704) [Ch 10, line 1710](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1710)
- **Reasoning:** with one overriding goal, general moral rules give way to expediency, and the posts that matter reward people willing to do what others will not. Because the plan's values must be justified by claims of fact, official doctrine spreads from values to facts, and words such as liberty keep their sound while changing their meaning. [Ch 10, line 1740](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1740) [Ch 11, line 1808](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1808) [Ch 11, line 1818](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1818)
- **Evidence:** a mechanism argument, illustrated from Nazi and Soviet practice (physics denounced on both sides, doubt treated as disloyalty in Soviet enterprises). [Ch 11, line 1832](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1832) [Ch 11, line 1826](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1826)
- **Boundary:** the examples all come from fully totalitarian states. Hayek does not show that partial planning sets off the same selection. **[Analysis]**
- **Application [Application]:** watch for goals declared important enough to suspend ordinary rules, and for old words being given new meanings.
