If the state plans the economy for the sake of distributive justice, by what principle will it decide who gets what, and who will do the deciding? Hayek's answer: no agreed principle exists short of absolute equality, which nobody wants; so the distribution will be decided by whoever holds the coercive power, every economic question becomes a political one, and the struggle for that power among groups with rival ideas of their due station is what produced fascism and National Socialism out of socialism.
