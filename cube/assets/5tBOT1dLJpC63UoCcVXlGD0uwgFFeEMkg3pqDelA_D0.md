**Nazism grew out of socialist and anti-liberal ideas, not against them.** This is the historical claim, and the least well supported. [Introduction, line 932](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L932)

---

- **Author's claim:** fascism and Nazism were an outcome of earlier socialist trends, carried by former socialists and united with conservatives by hostility to liberalism. The explanation lies in ideas, not German character. [Introduction, line 932](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L932) [Ch 12, line 1890](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1890) [Introduction, line 944](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L944)
- **Reasoning:** a genealogy of writers from 1914 onward (Sombart, Plenge, Lensch, Spengler, Moeller van den Bruck) who identified organisation and Prussianism with socialism, followed by a claim that British opinion in the 1940s resembles German opinion a generation earlier. [Ch 12, line 1896](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1896) [Ch 13, line 2044](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L2044)
- **Evidence:** quotation from opponents. The editor's notes show several of Hayek's quotations are loose or altered, including ones from E. H. Carr and Lensch. [Ch 13, line 2140](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L2140) [Ch 12, line 1994](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1994)
- **Boundary:** Frank Knight, a sympathetic reader, called the history over-simplified, and the editor agrees in part. [Knight's report, line 2548](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L2548) [Caldwell, line 432](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L432) Hayek had already stated the thesis in his 1933 memo. [1933 memo, line 2516](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L2516)
- **Application [Application]:** take the general point that movements with good intentions can prepare ground for ones they detest. Do not rely on this book for the causes of Nazism.
