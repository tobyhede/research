**This is not a case for laissez faire.** Hayek accepts regulation that preserves competition, social insurance, a guaranteed minimum income and anti-cyclical policy. Minimum security can be provided outside and supplementary to the market system. [Ch 3, line 1136](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1136) [Ch 9, line 1606](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1606) [Ch 9, line 1608](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1608)

---

- **Author's claim:** a dogmatic attachment to laissez faire did the liberal cause great harm. Competition needs a deliberately built legal framework, and a wide field remains for the state. [Ch 1, line 998](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L998) [Ch 3, line 1138](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1138) [Ch 3, line 1142](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1142)
- **Reasoning:** the dividing line is between planning for competition and planning against it. Security against severe privation can be given to everyone outside the market. Security of a particular income or status can only be given to some, by shutting others out. [Ch 3, line 1148](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1148) [Ch 9, line 1606](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1606)
- **Evidence:** his own list of accepted measures. It covers limits on working hours, sanitary rules and bans on poisons; extensive social services; a guaranteed minimum; state-organised insurance for sickness and accident; disaster relief; and monetary policy against the business cycle. [Ch 3, line 1136](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1136) [Ch 9, line 1608](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1608) [Ch 9, line 1610](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1610) [Ch 9, line 1612](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1612) The analytical core is that a fixed share guaranteed to one group makes everyone else's share fluctuate more. [Ch 9, line 1640](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1640)
- **Boundary:** he leaves open the level of the minimum and raises, without answering, whether recipients should keep all the same liberties. [Ch 9, line 1608](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1608) In 1976 he withdrew some concessions without naming them. [1976 preface, line 902](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L902) **[Analysis]** It is therefore wrong to read chapter 9 as an endorsement of the modern welfare state, and equally wrong to read the book as opposing social provision.
- **Application [Application]:** prefer support paid as a general floor to support delivered by protecting particular jobs, prices or trades.

> “planning and competition can be combined only by planning for competition but not by planning against competition.” [Ch 3, line 1148](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1148)

> “some minimum of food, shelter, and clothing, sufficient to preserve health and the capacity to work, can be assured to everybody.” [Ch 9, line 1608](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1608)
