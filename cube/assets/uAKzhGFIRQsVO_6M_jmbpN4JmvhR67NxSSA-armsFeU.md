Why must a totalitarian (planned) system control not only action but belief, and not only beliefs about values but beliefs about facts? And what does that do to truth, language, science and the growth of reason? Continues from ch10's closing remark that selection by conformity bears on all the virtues grouped under truthfulness (p. 165).

Epigraph: E. H. Carr, that the nationalization of thought has gone pari passu with the nationalization of industry (p. 168). **Editor's correction**: Carr actually wrote nationalization of opinion, not of thought (p. 176, note 1).
