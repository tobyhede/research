**Defined at** p. p. 80

Two senses separated on purpose: the ends (social justice, greater equality, security) and the method (abolishing private enterprise and private ownership of the means of production in favour of a central planning body). The book attacks the method.

**Evolves / reused at**

- fm03 p. 59 (a climate of opinion shared by all parties)
- ch02 p. 74 (a historical movement, authoritarian in origin)
- ch08 p. 140 (extended: any deliberate sharing-out of wealth by state control, so fascism counts)
- ch12 p. 179 (broadest: any anticapitalist doctrine of organisation, Left or Right)
- fm02 p. 42 (1956: hot socialism, nearly dead)
- fm02 p. 55 (1976: the word now chiefly means redistribution through taxation and the welfare state)
