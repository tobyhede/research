**The Rule of Law is the test.** State action through general rules announced in advance leaves people free to plan their own lives. Discretionary decisions about particular people and industries do not. [Ch 6, line 1326](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1326)

---

- **Author's claim:** under the Rule of Law, government is bound by rules fixed and announced beforehand, so people can foresee how coercion will be used. Planning cannot bind itself this way, because it must decide particular cases by weighing particular interests. [Ch 6, line 1326](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1326) [Ch 6, line 1330](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1330)
- **Reasoning:** a formal rule is impartial because its makers cannot know whom it will help. A planner who does foresee the effects is choosing between people. Legality is not enough: a legislature can lawfully grant unlimited discretion. [Ch 6, line 1338](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1338) [Ch 6, line 1358](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1358)
- **Evidence:** the highway-code analogy, H. G. Wells's draft rights declaration with its welfare proviso, and an assertion about the erosion of the German Rechtsstaat before Hitler. [Ch 6, line 1332](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1332) [Ch 6, line 1366](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1366) [Ch 6, line 1344](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1344)
- **Boundary:** Hayek admits the ideal is never fully met, the line is hard to draw in practice, and the Rule of Law produces economic inequality. [Ch 6, line 1326](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1326) [Ch 6, line 1332](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1332) [Ch 6, line 1348](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L1348) In 1956 he called this the central chapter and too brief. [1956 foreword, line 804](https://github.com/tobyhede/research/blob/main/books/road-to-serfdom/source/source.txt#L804)
- **Application [Application]:** ask of any measure whether it is general, announced in advance, and blind to who benefits.
